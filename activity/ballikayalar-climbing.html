About Ballıkayalar

Ballıkayalar is a limestone canyon and climbing area in the Gebze district of Kocaeli, roughly 60–70 km from central Istanbul. It is one of the most frequently visited sport climbing destinations in the Marmara region, with a wide spread of single-pitch routes on quality limestone. The canyon walls offer everything from beginner-friendly slabs and jugs to technical crimpy faces and overhangs.

The approach is short from the road. Routes are bolted and well-maintained. The canyon provides partial shade in summer, making it workable even in warm weather.
