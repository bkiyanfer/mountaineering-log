Mountains Ahead

The peaks still on the list — from Aladağlar to Ağrı.

These are the mountains that are next. Some require only a free weekend and the right season; others demand years of preparation. The list is honest about where things stand.

Demirkazık
3,756 m · Aladağlar, Central Taurus
The highest and most iconic peak in the Aladağlar range — a natural next step after the Yıldızlar traverse. The technical north face is one of the finest alpine routes in Turkey. Significant prior Aladağlar experience already in place.
Planned Summer / Autumn
Kızılkaya
3,725 m · Aladağlar, Central Taurus
A striking pyramid-shaped peak in the Aladağlar. Often approached alongside Demirkazık. The standard route is a non-technical but exposed ridge walk.
Planned Summer
Kaçkar
3,937 m · Kaçkar Mountains, Eastern Black Sea
The highest peak of the Pontic Alps. Multi-day approach through alpine valleys, high plateaus, and glacial lakes. Challenging but non-technical on the standard route.
Planned Summer
Süphan
4,058 m · Eastern Anatolia, Van
A volcanic peak rising above Lake Van. Non-technical on the standard route, but the altitude above 4,000 m demands proper acclimatisation. Good stepping stone to higher objectives.
Planned Summer
Ağrı (Ararat)
5,137 m · Eastern Anatolia
The highest mountain in Turkey. Requires a permit, a licensed guide, and serious altitude preparation. Base camp at ~3,200 m; high camp at ~4,200 m. The long-term horizon goal.
Planned Summer
