Yıldızbaşı

3,685 m · Summer · 4 July 2026 · Aladağlar

▲

About the Summit

Yıldızbaşı (Star-Head) is one of the two Yıldız peaks in the Aladağlar range, reaching 3,685 metres. The peak sits within the central part of the massif, accessed most directly from the Oba Yeri campsite. It forms a pair with Yıldızbatı (Star-West), the slightly higher of the two, and both were reached on the same day.

This Ascent — 4 July 2026

The group set off from Oba Yeri at 04:00, having prepared everything the night before. The ascent was very steep throughout, on loose scree and sections of compact slab. Strong, cold wind made the summit cold. The views across the Aladağlar cirques were exceptional — the limestone walls and high couloirs of the range visible in every direction.

Phone signal is available at the summit (not Vodafone). The Garmin watch GPS was unreliable on the ridge — navigation was done by phone map. An extra base-layer shirt for the summit is strongly recommended; the wind at 3,685 m is significant.

The team arrived at Yıldızbaşı 1 hour 15 minutes ahead of the planned schedule, in good spirits. After a brief summit pause, the group continued to Yıldızbatı along the ridge.
