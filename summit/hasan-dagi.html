Hasan Dağı

3,268 m · Winter Ascent · February 2026 · Central Anatolia

About the Summit

Hasan Dağı is a stratovolcano rising to 3,268 metres on the boundary of Aksaray and Niğde provinces in Central Anatolia. It stands as one of the most visually striking peaks on the central Anatolian plateau, visible from vast distances across the steppe. The summit is reached by a non-technical route in summer, but in winter conditions the mountain takes on a fully alpine character.

This Ascent — February 2026

This was a winter ascent completed in February 2026. Cold, clear conditions prevailed throughout. The volcanic summit plateau was dressed in deep snow, and the views across the Cappadocian plains were exceptional in the crisp winter air. The ascent via the north ridge approach was straightforward in terms of technical difficulty, though the cold and the altitude demanded steady movement.

A winter ascent of Hasan Dağı counts as the highest winter summit in the journal to date at 3,268 m.
