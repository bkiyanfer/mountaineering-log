About the Summit

Yıldızbatı (Star-West) is the slightly higher of the two Yıldız peaks in the Aladağlar, exceeding 3,700 metres. Together with Yıldızbaşı, it forms a compact summit pair on the Yıldız ridge. The traverse between the two peaks is more straightforward than the initial ascent, though exposed in places.

This Ascent — 4 July 2026

Yıldızbatı was the second summit of the day, reached from Yıldızbaşı via the connecting ridge. After the demanding first ascent in strong wind, the group's morale was high. The ridge between the two peaks was less technically demanding than the approach to Yıldızbaşı, though care was still needed on the loose sections and where the path drops close to a cliff edge on one side.

A rest of approximately one hour was taken at the summit — long enough to eat, recover, and enjoy the views before the second and more demanding section of the day: the descent into the valley and the traverse to Dipsiz Göl.

The return from Yıldızbatı was the most difficult part of the day: no visible trail under the snow, steep terrain in every direction, and a serious traverse section with a drop-off behind one of the slab steps. The group eventually split into two sub-groups and both lines reached the plateau safely, never losing sight of each other.

Field Notes

The descent from Yıldızbatı is the most technical part of the route. Snow coverage in early July makes the trail invisible. Keep groups in visual contact at all times on the descent. Snow depth was sufficient for glissading — check thickness before sliding.
