The journal, the person, and where this is all heading.

Beril Kiyanfer

This journal is a personal archive. It records the mountains I've climbed, the hikes I've walked, the climbs I've done on rock, and the experiences — the decisions, the weather, the moments on summit and in camp — that come with each of them.

The journal is public because the mountains are public. Anyone planning a route in the Aladağlar, considering Ballıkayalar for the first time, or wondering what early-July conditions look like on the Yıldız ridge might find something useful here. That's enough reason to write things down properly.

Where I've Been

I've reached four peaks so far: Hasan Dağı in winter (3,268 m), Emler (3,723 m), and Yıldızlar — the connected Yıldızbaşı (3,685 m) and Yıldızbatı (3,700+ m) summits — in the Aladağlar in the summer of 2026. My highest summit to date is Emler.

I also climb rock. Ballıkayalar near Kocaeli is a regular destination — good limestone, short approach, and a wide spread of routes.

Where I'm Going

The immediate list: Demirkazık and Kızılkaya in the Aladağlar, Kaçkar in the eastern Black Sea, and Süphan above Lake Van. Further out: Uludoruk in Hakkari, and eventually Ağrı. Each one requires something — fitness, preparation, logistics, a permit — and building toward them is part of what makes this worth doing.

This Journal

Every record here aims to be accurate: what happened, when, with whom, under what conditions, and what went wrong as much as what went right. A summit is a peak reached; an activity is a day in the mountains; a goal is honest about where things stand.

The site is managed through GitHub. No logins, no social features — just a clean public record of the journey as it unfolds.
