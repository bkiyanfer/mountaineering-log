Gallery

Photos and videos from summits, hikes, camps, and climbing days.

How to add photos or videos:
1. Drop the file into public/media/gallery/ (or the relevant sub-folder).
2. Copy one of the commented-out examples below, uncomment it, and update the src, alt, data-filter, and caption.
3. For videos, drop the file into public/media/videos/ and use a <video> tag instead of <img>.
